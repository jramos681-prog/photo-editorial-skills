# Bibliography

Citation numbers in this skill refer to the page printed in the book, never to the page of the PDF file: only the printed number locates the page in a physical copy. Literal quotations belong to their authors and publishers; they are reproduced as short quotations with attribution and are not covered by the CC BY 4.0 license (see `LICENSE-docs`).

`SKILL.md` names the authors of these six works as the sources the skill proceeds from. Each entry records the work as the skill states it, its citation prefix, and the reference files that use that prefix.
